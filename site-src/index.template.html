Medical oncology

Chalk Talks

Short, visual treatment conversations for people newly diagnosed with cancer. Walk through one together in the visit, then take it home as a handout.

  1. Open the talk that fits the decision.
  2. Fill in the details and mark it up together, step by step.
  3. Print the filled copy to take home, or a blank one to write on.

No talks match that search.

Anything typed into a talk (names, notes, plans) stays in that browser tab and is never sent or saved. Use "Print filled" before closing the tab if the patient should take it home.

Estimates in each talk come from the trials it cites and describe groups of people. They support, and do not replace, the conversation with the care team.
